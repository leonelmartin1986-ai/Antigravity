## Verbos en los 11 tiempos verbales

Cada verbo con sujeto I y she, en afirmativa, negativa y pregunta, con su traducción. En la app, el conjugador de cada palabra muestra los 6 sujetos.

### have (tener / haber)

| Tiempo | Nivel | Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Presente simple | Básico | I have breakfast every day.<br>_Yo desayuno todos los días._ | She doesn't have breakfast every day.<br>_Ella no desayuna todos los días._ | Does she have breakfast every day?<br>_¿Ella desayuna todos los días?_ |
| Pasado simple | Básico | I had breakfast yesterday.<br>_Yo desayuné ayer._ | She didn't have breakfast yesterday.<br>_Ella no desayunó ayer._ | Did she have breakfast yesterday?<br>_¿Ella desayunó ayer?_ |
| Presente perfecto | Básico | I have had breakfast before.<br>_Yo he desayunado antes._ | She hasn't had breakfast before.<br>_Ella no ha desayunado antes._ | Has she had breakfast before?<br>_¿Ella ha desayunado antes?_ |
| Presente continuo | Básico | I am having breakfast right now.<br>_Yo estoy desayunando ahora mismo._ | She isn't having breakfast right now.<br>_Ella no está desayunando ahora mismo._ | Is she having breakfast right now?<br>_¿Ella está desayunando ahora mismo?_ |
| Futuro (will) | Básico | I will have breakfast tomorrow.<br>_Yo desayunaré mañana._ | She won't have breakfast tomorrow.<br>_Ella no desayunará mañana._ | Will she have breakfast tomorrow?<br>_¿Ella desayunará mañana?_ |
