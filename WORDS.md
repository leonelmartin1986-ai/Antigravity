## Verbos en cada tiempo verbal

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Presente simple | I have breakfast every day. / She has breakfast every day. |
| Pasado simple | Yesterday I had breakfast. / Yesterday she had breakfast. |
| Presente perfecto | I have had breakfast before. / She has had breakfast before. |
| Presente continuo | I am having breakfast right now. / She is having breakfast right now. |
| Futuro | Tomorrow I will have breakfast. / Tomorrow she will have breakfast. |
